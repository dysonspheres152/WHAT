Security and privacy

This page describes how DYSON V1 works. If you publish DYSON, have a lawyer review it and replace it with your own policy.

What we store

  • Your email and password hash, managed by Supabase Auth.
  • Your profile: name, username, photo, bio, status.
  • Your messages and read state.
  • Presence: whether you are online and when you were last seen, if you allow it.

How access is limited

  • Row Level Security means you can only read conversations you are a member of.
  • You can only send messages as yourself, and only into your conversations.
  • Only you can edit your profile or see your notifications.

Important limits in V1

  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Anyone with administrator access to the Supabase project could read them. End-to-end encryption is planned for V4.
  • Profiles are visible to signed-in users so people can find you. Profile visibility controls are planned for V2.

Your controls

In Settings you can hide your online status and last seen.
