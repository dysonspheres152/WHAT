Help

How do I start a conversation?

Open DYSON, choose New, search for a name or username, then select Message.

Why do I see "Offline" for someone?

DYSON only shows Online while their app is open and sending a heartbeat. People can also hide their status in Settings.

I did not receive a confirmation email.

Check spam. The project owner can turn email confirmation off in Supabase under Authentication.

Can I send images or files?

Not yet. Image and file sharing is planned for V2.

How do I reset my password?

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link.

Are my messages end-to-end encrypted?

Not in V1. End-to-end encryption is planned for V4. See Security and privacy.
